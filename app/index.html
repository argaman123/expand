Set up this Mac
Three things, once. Each button opens the right place in System Settings.
  1. Screen Recording So your PC can show this Mac's extra display.
  2. Accessibility So your PC's mouse and keyboard can control this Mac.
  3. Streaming engine Sunshine sends the display to your PC.

When it's done, Sidescreen waits in the menu bar. Open Sidescreen on your PC and press Connect.
